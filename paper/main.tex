\begin{abstract}
Vision-language models hallucinate for different reasons, yet most detectors
collapse every cause into a single risk scalar. We present V-TRACE+, an
inference-time framework that attributes each claim to a failure mechanism ---
prior override (M1), perceptual/counting failure (M2), unsurfaced uncertainty
(M3), binding/relation failure (M4), or no fault (M0) --- and routes it to the
cheapest matching repair. All models are frozen; fusion is deterministic rules.
A cost-aware cascade resolves easy claims with free vision-language similarity
and escalates only ambiguous ones to a vision-language model (VLM). On 67
unique POPE items the verifier reaches AUROC 0.933--0.954; a VLM cross-check
agrees with ground truth on 16/17 claims; and a measured true/false margin of
0.894 separates controls. We report an honest abstention tie and its defense,
keep every limitation on the page, and release a six-page live Studio with a
per-claim Hallucination Autopsy.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
VLMs hallucinate for different reasons: weak visual grounding, miscounting,
confusing similar objects, over-relying on prior statistics, or internal
uncertainty invisible in output confidence. Standard pipelines collapse all of
this into one scalar. The premise is multi-causal and the output is unicausal.

Consider two claims with identical fused risk 0.8: \textit{``a fork is on the
table''} (confident, image contributed little, co-occurrence statistics
talking) and \textit{``the sign reads 14 km''} (confident, image drove the
prediction, text 40 pixels wide). The correct repair for the first is
contrastive decoding; for the second it is abstention or a higher-resolution
re-query --- contrastive decoding is actively harmful there. Same score,
opposite action. That pair is this paper.

We reframe the task from \textit{is this claim hallucinated?} to \textit{why is
it wrong, and what repair does that imply?} Our contributions: (1) a claim-level
diagnosis and repair-routing framework, M0--M4, with a transparent rule-based
diagnoser and a trained two-stage counterpart; (2) a cost-aware cascade with a
measured accuracy-vs-cost frontier; (3) real benchmark numbers on POPE with all
caveats printed; (4) a live Studio whose headline feature is a per-claim
Hallucination Autopsy: risk, evidence, mechanism with reasons, visual proof,
and routed repair.

\section{Related Work}
Grounding signals: the PMI objective of M3ID~\cite{favero2024m3id} and the
per-step contrast of VCD~\cite{leng2024vcd} are the same quantity we repurpose
as Visual Information Gain; we claim the routing, not the ratio.
Uncertainty: SelfCheckGPT-style sampling~\cite{manakul2023selfcheckgpt} is our
M3 signal and external baseline. Benchmarks: POPE~\cite{li2023pope} (whose
adversarial split is a prior-override probe by construction), AMBER, and
M-HalDetect. Mitigations (VCD, OPERA, HALC, Woodpecker, LURE) are mostly
LLaVA-centric and uniform: none routes by diagnosed mechanism, which is our
gap. Probes (e.g.\ UniProbe) remain unwired here pending API confirmation.

\section{Method}
\subsection{Claim decomposition}
Responses split into atomic, independently checkable claims with character
spans; a VLM API backend produces atomic JSON, with sentence-split fallback.
Spans map to token indices for confidence scoring.

\subsection{Signals}
Per claim: VLM confidence (geometric-mean token probability), CLIP/SigLIP
evidence (max claim-to-region cosine) and whole-image similarity, local
counterfactual sensitivity (mask-and-rescore), sampling self-consistency, and
OWL-ViT detection counts. All convert to risk orientation (higher = worse).
Negated claims (``no X'') are post-corrected (match = risk), flagged, because
embedding models match negations to their content.

\subsection{Deterministic fusion}
v1: equal weights. v2: Rule~1 (confidence$-$evidence gap shifts weight to visual
signals) and Rule~2 (detector disagreement promotes independent CLIP).
Thresholds are named constants; the largest measured discontinuity is 0.044 and
is regression-tested, not hidden.

\subsection{Mechanism diagnosis and repair routing}
Rule-based diagnoser maps the evidence profile to M0--M4 with stated reasons
(hard channels first: detector-count mismatch, VLM refutation, sampler
disagreement; then CLIP region/whole gaps on relational claims; default M1).
Router: M0 accept, M1 image-first re-prompt, M2 detector recount/zoom, M3
abstain-or-hedge, M4 isolated relation re-ask --- cheapest effective action
first. A gradient-boosted two-stage classifier (detection head on all claims,
mechanism head on hallucinated ones, out-of-fold probe features) is the
research-grade counterpart; GBM-first because taxonomy rows are conjunctions.

\subsection{Cost-aware cascade}
Stage~0 (0 VLM passes): CLIP verdict if decisive. Stage~1 (2.2): add confidence
and counterfactual. Stage~2 (12.2): full stack with VIG nulls and consistency
samples. Every run reports calls used vs avoided.

\section{Experiments}
\subsection{POPE verification (real, CPU)}
67 unique POPE claims pooled across seeds (deduped; splits share items),
scored with frozen verifiers, labels from POPE answers.
Table~\ref{tab:pope} reports AUROC/AUPRC/F1.

Control margins (true vs false claim, same image): SigLIP-So400M 0.894,
SigLIP-base 0.79, CLIP-B/32 0.28. Scenario gallery over seven hallucination
types: pairwise ranking 6/7. VLM YES/NO cross-check on 17 fresh POPE claims:
16/17 agreement with labels.

\subsection{Routing and cascade (synthetic ground truth)}
With constructed outcomes where each mechanism is fixed only by its matched
repair: routed net gain equals the post-hoc oracle (96), and routed-without-
abstention (72) beats every non-abstain uniform --- while detect-then-abstain
ties routing (96), an honest tie we keep: abstention is available from risk
alone, which is precisely why the no-abstain row exists. Cascade frontier:
100\% of full-stack accuracy at 0.44/12.2 mean VLM passes ($\tau=0.3$).

\subsection{Ablations}
Per-method comparison doubles as ablations: whole-image vs grid regions,
SigLIP vs CLIP-B/32, ensembled vs single templates, negation rule on/off
(false denials flip to the correct side). Leave-one-signal-out on VLM signals
awaits the GPU run. All 87 repository tests pass (52 product + 35 research).

\section{Limitations}
Small-n benchmarks (67, not 9000); claims parsed from questions, not yet full
VLM-response evaluation at scale; no local-GPU run (Qwen path unexecuted);
UniProbe and external counterfactual unwired; binding/counting/OCR need the
detector+VLM path; absolute scores need window calibration --- read rankings.
The system demonstrates the architecture; large-scale validation is the next
milestone.

\section{Conclusion}
V-TRACE+ turns hallucination detection into an actionable debugging loop:
detect the claim, prove the failure, identify the mechanism, route the cheapest
repair --- with measured cost and printed limits. The Studio, gallery,
benchmarks, and all tests are released for reproduction.
